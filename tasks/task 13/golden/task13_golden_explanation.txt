### Overview

The zip is a complete Lake project. `lakefile.toml` and `lake-manifest.json` use the pinned Mathlib checkout at `/opt/mathlib4` (commit `9944fe2973b8dc0b86949101ed98232c07cd54a0`), and `lean-toolchain` is `leanprover/lean4:v4.33.0-rc1`. The proof is split into four files under `TuringTask/` that import each other in a chain: `Fibre`, `Resultants`, `Discr`, `Basic`. The required file `TuringTask/Basic.lean` is the file of the prompt: `import Mathlib`, the namespace and the five statements are unchanged, and each `sorry` is replaced by a one-line call of the matching theorem in `Resultants.lean` or `Discr.lean`.

Notation: $\Phi_n$ is the $n$-th cyclotomic polynomial over $\mathbb{Z}$, $\varphi$ is Euler's function, $S_n$ is the set of primitive $n$-th roots of unity in $\mathbb{C}$ (`primitiveRoots n C`, with $\varphi(n)$ elements), and $\mathrm{Res}(f,g)$ is `Polynomial.resultant f g` with the default sizes $\deg f$, $\deg g$. For $n \ge 1$ put $\Psi_n = \prod_{d \mid n,\ d < n} \Phi_d$, so that $\Phi_n \Psi_n = X^n - 1$.

The route:
- Every resultant is evaluated in $\mathbb{C}$ through $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_m, g) = \prod_{\alpha \in S_m} g(\alpha)$ (Mathlib's `resultant_eq_prod_eval`; $\Phi_m$ is monic and its complex roots are exactly $S_m$).
- Fibre lemma: for $m, d \ge 1$ and $k = m / \gcd(m,d)$, the map $\alpha \mapsto \alpha^d$ sends $S_m$ onto $S_k$ and every fibre has $\varphi(m)/\varphi(k)$ elements. Hence $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_m, G(X^d)) = \mathrm{Res}(\Phi_k, G)^{\varphi(m)/\varphi(k)}$ for every integer polynomial $G$. With $G = X - 1$ this gives $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_m, X^n - 1) = \big((-1)^{\varphi(k)} \Phi_k(1)\big)^{\varphi(m)/\varphi(k)}$, which is theorem 5.
- Theorem 1 ($m/n$ not a prime power): the resultant is a non-zero integer (coprimality over $\mathbb{Q}$); no prime $q$ divides it, because a common root of $\Phi_m$ and $\Phi_n$ in an algebraic closure of $\mathbb{F}_q$ would be a primitive $m'$-th and a primitive $n'$-th root of unity, where $m = q^a m'$, $n = q^b n'$ with $q \nmid m', n'$, forcing $m' = n'$ and $m = n q^{a-b}$; so the resultant is $\pm 1$, and it is $\ge 0$ because the primitive roots come in conjugate pairs when $m \ge 3$.
- Theorem 2: for $p \nmid n$, $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{np^k}, X^n - 1) = \prod_{d \mid n} \mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{np^k}, \Phi_d)$, all factors with $d < n$ equal $1$ by theorem 1, and the left side is known from the fibre lemma; the general case $p \mid n$ is reduced to this one through `expand`.
- Theorem 4 follows from theorems 1 and 2 and Mathlib's `isUnit_resultant_iff_isCoprime`; the exceptional pair $(2,1)$ has $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_2, \Phi_1) = -2$.
- Theorem 3: $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_n, \Psi_n)$ satisfies the recursion $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{np}, \Psi_{np}) = \mathrm{Res}(\Phi_n, \Psi_n)^{p-1}\, \mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{np}, \Phi_n)$ for $p \nmid n$ and $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{np^v}, \Psi_{np^v}) = \mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{np}, \Psi_{np})^{p^{v-1}}$, which gives $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_n, \Psi_n) = \prod_{p \mid n} p^{\varphi(n)/(p-1)}$ for $n \ge 3$. Differentiating $\Phi_n \Psi_n = X^n - 1$ and evaluating at $S_n$ gives $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_n, \Phi_n')\, \mathrm{Res}(\Phi_n, \Psi_n) = n^{\varphi(n)}$, and Mathlib's `resultant_deriv` turns $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_n, \Phi_n')$ into the discriminant.

### 1. Fibre.lean: the fibre lemma

- `exists_coprime_lift`: a residue coprime to $k$ lifts to a residue coprime to $m$ when $k \mid m$ (from `ZMod.unitsMap_surjective`).
- `primitiveRoot_pow_eq_pow_of_modEq`, `pow_mem_primitiveRoots`, `pow_injOn_primitiveRoots`: for $\alpha \in S_m$, exponents congruent modulo $m$ give the same power, $\alpha^d \in S_{m/\gcd(m,d)}$ (`orderOf_pow'`), and $\alpha \mapsto \alpha^u$ is injective on $S_m$ for $u$ coprime to $m$.
- `prod_pow_primitiveRoots`: $\prod_{\alpha \in S_m} F(\alpha^d) = \big(\prod_{\eta \in S_k} F(\eta)\big)^{\varphi(m)/\varphi(k)}$. The product is grouped by the value $\eta = \alpha^d$ (`Finset.prod_fiberwise_of_maps_to`). Two fibres over $\eta_1, \eta_2 \in S_k$ have the same size: write $\eta_2 = \eta_1^v$ with $v$ coprime to $k$, lift $v$ to $u$ coprime to $m$; then $\alpha \mapsto \alpha^u$ maps the fibre of $\eta_1$ injectively into the fibre of $\eta_2$, and symmetrically. Since the fibres partition $S_m$, each has $\varphi(m)/\varphi(k)$ elements (`Finset.card_eq_sum_card_fiberwise`, `Complex.card_primitiveRoots`).
- `resultant_cyclotomic_eq_prod`: $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_m, g) = \prod_{\alpha \in S_m} g(\alpha)$ in $\mathbb{C}$ for every $g \in \mathbb{Z}[X]$ (`resultant_map_map`, `map_cyclotomic_int`, `resultant_eq_prod_eval`, `cyclotomic.roots_eq_primitiveRoots_val`).
- `resultant_cyclotomic_expand`: $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_m, \mathrm{expand}_d\, G) = \mathrm{Res}(\Phi_k, G)^{\varphi(m)/\varphi(k)}$, from the two previous results and `expand_eval`.

### 2. Resultants.lean: theorems 1, 2, 4 and 5

- `resultant_cyclotomic_X_pow_sub_one_eq`: $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_m, X^n - 1) = \big((-1)^{\varphi(k)} \Phi_k(1)\big)^{\varphi(m)/\varphi(k)}$ with $k = m/\gcd(m,n)$, since $X^n - 1 = \mathrm{expand}_n (X - 1)$ and $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_k, X - 1) = (-1)^{\varphi(k)} \Phi_k(1)$ (`resultant_X_sub_C_right`).
- `prod_conj_pairs_nonneg`, `conj_mem_primitiveRoots`, `im_ne_zero_of_mem_primitiveRoots`, `resultant_cyclotomic_nonneg`: a finite set of complex numbers that is closed under conjugation and contains no real number splits into the elements with positive and with negative imaginary part, in conjugate pairs; for a function commuting with conjugation the product over the set is $\prod |f(z)|^2 \ge 0$. For $m \ge 3$ the set $S_m$ has these properties (a real root of unity has order at most $2$, `LinearOrderedRing.orderOf_le_two`), and evaluation of an integer polynomial commutes with conjugation (`eval_map_conj`). Hence $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_m, g) \ge 0$ for all $g \in \mathbb{Z}[X]$.
- `resultant_cyclotomic_ne_zero`: $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_m, \Phi_n) \ne 0$ for $m \ne n$ (`cyclotomic.isCoprime_rat`, `resultant_eq_zero_iff` over $\mathbb{Q}$).
- `not_prime_dvd_resultant_cyclotomic`: if a prime $q$ divided $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_m, \Phi_n)$, the resultant would vanish over $K = $ `AlgebraicClosure (ZMod q)`, so $\Phi_m$ and $\Phi_n$ would share a root $\mu \in K$ (`resultant_eq_zero_iff`, `isCoprime_iff_aeval_ne_zero_of_isAlgClosed`). Writing $m = q^a m'$ and $n = q^b n'$ with $q \nmid m', n'$ (`Nat.exists_eq_pow_mul_and_not_dvd`), the roots of $\Phi_{q^a m'}$ in characteristic $q$ are the primitive $m'$-th roots of unity (`isRoot_cyclotomic_prime_pow_mul_iff_of_charP`), so $\mu$ is a primitive $m'$-th and a primitive $n'$-th root of unity, $m' = n'$ (`IsPrimitiveRoot.unique`), and $n < m$ gives $b < a$ and $m = n q^{a-b}$, excluded by the hypothesis.
- `resultant_cyclotomic_eq_one_of_not_prime_pow` (theorem 1): the integer is non-zero and has no prime factor, so its absolute value is $1$ (`Nat.eq_one_iff_not_exists_prime_dvd`); the hypothesis excludes $(m,n) = (2,1)$, so $m \ge 3$ and the resultant is non-negative, hence equal to $1$.
- `resultant_cyclotomic_X_pow_sub_one_eq_prod_divisors`: $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_m, X^n - 1) = \prod_{d \mid n} \mathrm{Res}(\Phi_m, \Phi_d)$ (`prod_cyclotomic_eq_X_pow_sub_one`, `resultant_prod_right`).
- `not_eq_mul_prime_pow_of_properDivisor`: for $p \nmid n$, $k \ge 1$ and a proper divisor $d$ of $n$, $np^k$ is never $d q^j$ with $q$ prime (such a $q$ would be $p$, and then $n/d$ would be a power of $p$ dividing $n$).
- `resultant_cyclotomic_mul_prime_pow_coprime_signed`: for $p \nmid n$, $k \ge 1$: $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{np^k}, \Phi_n) = (-1)^{\varphi(p^k)\varphi(n)} p^{\varphi(n)}$. In the divisor product the factors with $d < n$ are $1$ by theorem 1, and the left side is $\big((-1)^{\varphi(p^k)} \Phi_{p^k}(1)\big)^{\varphi(n)}$ with $\Phi_{p^k}(1) = p$ (`eval_one_cyclotomic_prime_pow`), because $\gcd(np^k, n) = n$ and $\varphi(np^k)/\varphi(p^k) = \varphi(n)$.
- `resultant_cyclotomic_mul_prime_pow_coprime`: the sign is $+1$ outside $(n,p,k) = (1,2,1)$: either $p^k > 2$ and $\varphi(p^k)$ is even, or $p^k = 2$, $n$ is odd and $n \ge 3$, so $\varphi(n)$ is even (`Nat.totient_even`).
- `cyclotomic_expand_pow`: $\mathrm{expand}_{p^j} \Phi_n = \Phi_{np^j}$ when $p \mid n$ (iterating `cyclotomic_expand_eq_cyclotomic`).
- `resultant_cyclotomic_mul_prime_pow_once`: for $p \nmid n'$ and $k \ge 1$, $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{n'p^{k+1}}, \Phi_{n'p}) = p^{\varphi(n'p)}$. From $\mathrm{expand}_p \Phi_{n'} = \Phi_{n'p}\Phi_{n'}$ (`cyclotomic_expand_eq_cyclotomic_mul`) and `resultant_mul_right`: $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{n'p^{k+1}}, \mathrm{expand}_p \Phi_{n'}) = \mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{n'p^{k+1}}, \Phi_{n'p})\, \mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{n'p^{k+1}}, \Phi_{n'})$. The left side is $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{n'p^k}, \Phi_{n'})^p = \big((-1)^{\varphi(p^k)\varphi(n')} p^{\varphi(n')}\big)^p$ by `resultant_cyclotomic_expand`, the sign disappears because $\varphi(p^k) p$ is even, and the second factor on the right is $p^{\varphi(n')}$ (exponent $k + 1 \ge 2$, no exception). Cancelling $p^{\varphi(n')}$ gives $p^{(p-1)\varphi(n')} = p^{\varphi(n'p)}$.
- `resultant_cyclotomic_mul_prime_pow_eq` (theorem 2): write $n = p^v n'$ with $p \nmid n'$. For $v = 0$ this is the coprime case. For $v = w + 1$: $\Phi_n = \mathrm{expand}_{p^w} \Phi_{n'p}$ and $\Phi_{np^k} = \mathrm{expand}_{p^w} \Phi_{n'p^{k+1}}$, so by `resultant_cyclotomic_expand` with $d = p^w$ (here $\gcd(np^k, p^w) = p^w$ and $\varphi(np^k)/\varphi(n'p^{k+1}) = p^w$) the resultant is $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{n'p^{k+1}}, \Phi_{n'p})^{p^w} = p^{\varphi(n'p) p^w} = p^{\varphi(n)}$.
- `isCoprime_cyclotomic_iff'` (theorem 4): by `isUnit_resultant_iff_isCoprime` ($\Phi_m$ is monic) coprimality means that the resultant is a unit of $\mathbb{Z}$. If $m = np^k$ ($k \ge 1$ because $n < m$) the resultant is $p^{\varphi(n)}$ by theorem 2, or $-2$ for $(n,p,k) = (1,2,1)$ (`resultant_X_sub_C_right`); neither is a unit. Conversely theorem 1 gives the value $1$.
- `resultant_cyclotomic_X_pow_sub_one_eval` (theorem 5): from `resultant_cyclotomic_X_pow_sub_one_eq` with $k = n/\gcd(n,m)$; the sign $(-1)^{\varphi(k) \cdot \varphi(n)/\varphi(k)} = (-1)^{\varphi(n)}$ is $1$ for $n \ge 3$ (`Nat.totient_dvd_of_dvd`, `Nat.totient_even`). The hypothesis $n \nmid m$ of the task is not needed for the identity (when $n \mid m$ both sides are $0$); it is kept as an unused argument.

### 3. Discr.lean: theorem 3

- `cyclotomic_mul_prod_properDivisors`: $\Phi_n \Psi_n = X^n - 1$. `expand_X_pow_sub_one`: $\mathrm{expand}_p (X^n - 1) = X^{np} - 1$.
- `prod_properDivisors_cyclotomic_mul_prime`: $\Psi_{np} = (\mathrm{expand}_p \Psi_n) \Phi_n$ for $p \nmid n$, and `prod_properDivisors_cyclotomic_mul_prime_dvd`, `prod_properDivisors_cyclotomic_mul_prime_pow`: $\Psi_{np^j} = \mathrm{expand}_{p^j} \Psi_n$ for $p \mid n$. Both come from applying $\mathrm{expand}_p$ to $\Phi_n \Psi_n = X^n - 1$ and cancelling $\Phi_{np}$ in $\mathbb{Z}[X]$.
- `resultant_cyclotomic_prod_properDivisors_prime_pow`: $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{np^v}, \Psi_{np^v}) = \mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{np}, \Psi_{np})^{p^{v-1}}$ for $p \nmid n$, $v \ge 1$ (`resultant_cyclotomic_expand` with $d = p^{v-1}$).
- `resultant_cyclotomic_prod_properDivisors_prime`: $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{np}, \Psi_{np}) = \mathrm{Res}(\Phi_n, \Psi_n)^{p-1}\, \mathrm{Res}(\Phi_{np}, \Phi_n)$ for $p \nmid n$ (`resultant_mul_right` and `resultant_cyclotomic_expand` with $d = p$, where $\varphi(np)/\varphi(n) = p - 1$).
- `resultant_cyclotomic_prod_properDivisors_sq`: $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_n, \Psi_n)^2 = \big(\prod_{p \mid n} p^{\varphi(n)/(p-1)}\big)^2$ for all $n \ge 1$, by induction over the prime factorisation (`Nat.recOnPrimePow`). For $n = 1$ both sides are $1$ (`resultant_one_right`). For $n = p^v a$ with $p \nmid a$ the two recursions and `resultant_cyclotomic_mul_prime_pow_coprime_signed` give $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_n, \Psi_n) = \big(\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_a, \Psi_a)^{p-1} (\pm 1) p^{\varphi(a)}\big)^{p^{v-1}}$; squaring removes the sign, and the right side is rearranged with `Nat.primeFactors_mul`, `Nat.primeFactors_prime_pow`, $\varphi(p^v a) = \varphi(a) p^{v-1}(p-1)$ and $(q - 1) \mid \varphi(a)$ for the primes $q \mid a$. Squares are used so that the exceptional value $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_2, \Psi_2) = -2$ needs no special treatment.
- `resultant_cyclotomic_prod_properDivisors_eq`: for $n \ge 3$ both sides are non-negative (`resultant_cyclotomic_nonneg`), so $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_n, \Psi_n) = \prod_{p \mid n} p^{\varphi(n)/(p-1)}$ (Mathlib: `sq_eq_sq` with the subscript zero, for non-negative arguments).
- `prod_primitiveRoots_eq_one_of_three_le`: $\prod_{\alpha \in S_n} \alpha = 1$ for $n \ge 3$, from $\Phi_n = \prod_{\alpha \in S_n} (X - \alpha)$ over $\mathbb{C}$, $\Phi_n(0) = 1$ (`cyclotomic_coeff_zero`) and $\varphi(n)$ even.
- `resultant_derivative_mul_resultant_prod_properDivisors`: $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_n, \Phi_n')\, \mathrm{Res}(\Phi_n, \Psi_n) = n^{\varphi(n)}$ for $n \ge 3$. Over $\mathbb{C}$ the left side is $\prod_{\alpha \in S_n} \Phi_n'(\alpha) \Psi_n(\alpha)$; differentiating $\Phi_n \Psi_n = X^n - 1$ (`derivative_mul`, `derivative_X_pow`) and evaluating at a root $\alpha$ of $\Phi_n$ gives $\Phi_n'(\alpha) \Psi_n(\alpha) = n \alpha^{n-1}$, and $\prod_{\alpha} n\alpha^{n-1} = n^{\varphi(n)}$ by the previous lemma.
- `discr_cyclotomic'` (theorem 3): for $n = 1, 2$ the polynomial is linear, so its discriminant is $1$ (`discr_of_degree_eq_one`) and both sides are checked directly. For $n \ge 3$, `resultant_deriv` gives $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_n, \Phi_n') = (-1)^{e}\, \mathrm{discr}(\Phi_n)$ with $e = \varphi(n)(\varphi(n) - 1)/2$ (the sizes agree by `natDegree_derivative`), so $\mathrm{discr}(\Phi_n) \prod_{p \mid n} p^{\varphi(n)/(p-1)} = (-1)^e n^{\varphi(n)}$, and for $\varphi(n) = 2t$ the sign $(-1)^{t(2t-1)}$ equals $(-1)^t = (-1)^{\varphi(n)/2}$.

### Sources and related work

The value of $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_m, \Phi_n)$ ($p^{\varphi(n)}$ when $m/n$ is a power of the prime $p$, $1$ otherwise, for $m > n$) was first determined by F.-E. Diederichsen (Uber die Ausreduktion ganzzahliger Gruppendarstellungen bei arithmetischer Aquivalenz, Abhandlungen aus dem Mathematischen Seminar der Hansischen Universitat 13, 1940) and given a short proof by T. M. Apostol (Resultants of cyclotomic polynomials, Proceedings of the AMS 24, 1970, 457-462); Apostol's paper also contains the evaluation of $\prod_{\alpha \in S_n} (\alpha^m - 1)$ behind theorem 5. The discriminant formula $\mathrm{disc}(\Phi_n) = (-1)^{\varphi(n)/2} n^{\varphi(n)} / \prod_{p \mid n} p^{\varphi(n)/(p-1)}$ is classical; see for example L. C. Washington, Introduction to Cyclotomic Fields (2nd edition, Springer, 1997), Proposition 2.7, and S. Lang, Algebra, Chapter VI, Exercise on cyclotomic polynomials. The proofs here are arranged around the fibre lemma for the power map on primitive roots and the recursion for $\mathrm{Res}(\Phi_n, \Psi_n)$; the write-up and the Lean code are my own.

Related Lean code: Mathlib at the pinned commit provides the resultant (`Polynomial.resultant`, `resultant_eq_prod_eval`, `resultant_deriv`, `isUnit_resultant_iff_isCoprime`), the discriminant `Polynomial.discr` (with closed forms only for degrees at most $3$), cyclotomic polynomials with `expand` identities and roots, and the field discriminant of the $p^k$-th cyclotomic field (`IsCyclotomicExtension.discr_prime_pow`), but none of the five statements. I found no Lean formalization of the resultant or discriminant formulas for general $n$ (Mathlib, TauCetiProject/TauCeti, AllenGrahamHart/FormalConjectures-Bench, riccardobrasca/KummerCriterion, web search). Two of the three model responses to this task also build on Mathlib's resultant API and on `ZMod.unitsMap` (seen in an automated scan of declaration names); the golden answer was written from the statements and the Mathlib sources without reading those proofs.
